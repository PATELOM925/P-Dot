# P-Dot short demo walkthrough

Status: prepared, not recorded or run against production. Use synthetic prompts only. Do not use a private mailbox, real customer data, personal memory or a production-connected account in a recording.

## Four-minute script

1. Open the landing page and identify the two approval modes. Use a local or otherwise approved demo environment with a synthetic account. Do not show a real email address or credential.
2. Open Settings → Approvals. Show Ask first and Auto. Explain that Auto can add at most three tasks and reminders from a reply, with Undo; other kinds still wait for approval.
3. In a synthetic chat, ask: “Remind me to water the demo basil plant tomorrow at 9 am.” Show the proposed reminder and its approval card. Approve only in the isolated demo account if the environment owner has approved the demo write.
4. Open Activity. Show the action metadata entry and explain that the Activity trail omits the reminder text. Open “What was sent” to show its context metadata.
5. Open Connectors and expand “What connected accounts can do.” Explain the read-only app tools, per-message source selection and the extra approval for Google sends or calendar additions. Do not connect a real account or perform an external action.
6. If the isolated demo has a synthetic connected source and a verified test flow, show the metadata-only source entry in Activity and the follow-up search boundary. Otherwise describe the screen without claiming a read occurred.
7. Close on the public Controls and evidence page. State that it is a partial source map, not a certification.
