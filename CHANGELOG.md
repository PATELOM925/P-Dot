# Changelog

All notable changes to P-Dot. Newest first. Each entry says what changed, the problem it solves, and how it was resolved, with the pull request and review links. Commit messages hold the line-level detail.

Every pull request adds its entry under **Unreleased**. When it merges, the entry moves under the merge date. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

Lessons about provider calls, retries, backups and spending are in [docs/PROVIDER-RELIABILITY.md](docs/PROVIDER-RELIABILITY.md).

## Unreleased

### Fixed
- **Calendar events get the right end, weekday and time zone, and citation codes no longer show.** Problem: in a live test, "10 am to 11 am" became an event from 10 am to 5 pm, a reply said "Thu Nov 4" for a Wednesday, a table said "Time (IST)" over -05:00 times and told the user to add 10.5 hours, and a reply showed `【calendar†L1-L2】`. Resolution: `propose_calendar_event` takes `start` and `durationMinutes` (default 60) and works out the end; it refuses an end that is not after the start, that does not agree with the length, or that is over 12 hours without "all day" in the user's message, and the model can try once more. The approval card shows the weekday, the time zone and the length. The system prompt lists today and the next 14 days with weekdays and offsets in the user's time zone, and says to show times in that zone and never ask the user to convert. `calendar_list_events` gives each event as "Wed 2026-11-04 10:00 to 11:00" in the user's time zone. The reply normalizer removes named citation codes, also in the stream. PROMPT_VERSION is 2026-10-30.9. ([PR #43](https://github.com/PATELOM925/P-Dot/pull/43))
- **A mail question without Gmail access says how to turn it on.** Problem: with only the calendar scope, "Any unread email from Sam?" got no line that Gmail was off (review of PR #40). Resolution: such a message gets a server line that makes the reply say "Gmail access is off. Open Connectors, connect Google again and tick Gmail." The "Do not search Gmail instead." words are now only in the calendar-only case. ([commit 0cbcb15](https://github.com/PATELOM925/P-Dot/commit/0cbcb15d9d79a899e69e45fc93a1befc093a0a17))
- **Chat uses your documents and memories without the words "my documents".** Problem: a user with a ready resume asked "What are the job roles I should apply to?"; the turn went to the fast tier with no documents, and the reply asked for their experience. "check it from my memory" also got no documents, and the reply used an unrelated memory about diet and background. Resolution: each chat turn reads the titles and sizes of the user's ready documents in the mode (one small read). When they total 12,000 characters or less, all their text goes into the turn by title, untrusted and wrapped, with no embedding call. When they are larger, the turn searches them once with the embedding the memory lookup already makes, and keeps the best matches (similarity 0.1 or more). Both show under "Documents" in "What was sent". A turn with documents runs on the standard tier, and the first spend hold prices the documents block. "my memory", "about me", "my profile", "my background", "my experience", "my skills", "based on me" and "for someone like me" now offer the own-data tools; "What is a resume?" does not. A new prompt rule: use a memory only when it is relevant, and never mention personal traits such as diet, religion, ethnicity or gender unless the question is about them. PROMPT_VERSION is 2026-10-30.8. ([commit 7ca1cdd](https://github.com/PATELOM925/P-Dot/commit/7ca1cdd50c481a28fab86d3f0307ee094b7cb722))
- **Sign-in buttons work after Back.** Problem: after a click on Sign in, Continue with Google or "Try P-Dot now" and then Back, the browser showed the page from its back/forward cache with the button still pending and disabled. Resolution: one shared hook in `src/lib/page-show.ts` listens for `pageshow` with `persisted` true; the email forms and the Google button remount, and the trial button resets its state. The Connectors Connect buttons are plain forms with no pending state, so they had no fault. ([commit e8d2eb5](https://github.com/PATELOM925/P-Dot/commit/e8d2eb5695850afc1ab1cd00d91ea7048b8b9eb0))

- **Gmail and Calendar chat tools with a "Use Google" toggle** ([decision 0014, section 3](docs/decisions/0014-google-sign-in-and-connection.md)). ([PR #30](https://github.com/PATELOM925/P-Dot/pull/30))
  - Problem: P-Dot could connect Google, but the assistant could not read the user's mail or calendar, or propose an email or an event. Email text can try to give the model instructions, and private text could leave through a web search query.
  - Resolution:
    - "Use Google" in the composer "+" menu turns on, for one message, five tools: Gmail search and read, the calendar list, and email and event proposals.
    - All Google text goes to the model in untrusted blocks, at most 12,000 characters in one turn.
    - Web search, Google and connected apps are exclusive for a message. The request schema, the tool check and the composer each refuse two of them together. Several apps together stay allowed.
    - A Google turn makes no web search. After a reply that read Google or a connected app, the model cannot write a search query in that conversation. A failed check counts as such a reply.
    - The server writes an email or event proposal only when the user's own message asks for it, not negated, not quoted and not a question, and every address was written by the user. Otherwise nothing is saved.
    - A credential that ended marks the connection for a reconnect, with a write that finishes after the response, and stops Google for the turn.
- **Read-only Notion, Linear and Todoist connectors in chat** ([decision 0016](docs/decisions/0016-mcp-connectors.md)). ([PR #31](https://github.com/PATELOM925/P-Dot/pull/31))
  - Problem: P-Dot cannot read the user's notes and tasks in Notion, Linear or Todoist. Their MCP servers can change their tool definitions, and text in a result can try to give the model instructions.
  - Resolution:
    - A small MCP client of our own (Streamable HTTP, JSON or SSE, size caps, timeouts, zod) and OAuth with discovery checked against a catalog, a Client ID Metadata Document at `/oauth/client-metadata.json` with dynamic registration as the fallback, PKCE, `resource` and `iss`. The tokens are sealed with AES-256-GCM under the new secret `CONNECTOR_TOKEN_ENCRYPTION_KEY`.
    - Two fixed read tools for each app, with names, descriptions and arguments that P-Dot writes. At Connect, P-Dot pins a hash of each server tool's name and input schema. A changed tool is refused before the call.
    - The refresh is fenced by the ciphertext and `status = 'connected'`: a disconnect, a reconnect or a reconnect mark makes a late refresh match no row. An unknown refresh result never sends the old refresh token again. Disconnect deletes only the row it read.
    - "Use <app>" in the chat "+" menu, for one message. A search argument must be one phrase of the user's message, once for each app in a turn. An ID must come from a typed field of this app's results in this turn, or from the user's message. Results go to the model in untrusted blocks, at most 12,000 characters for each turn. Such a turn has no web search, and a conversation with such a reply never offers the model-written web search again.
    - Anonymous demo sessions are refused on every route and before the chat quota. A card for each app on Connectors, with health flags.
- **Database step for the Notion, Linear and Todoist connectors** ([decision 0016](docs/decisions/0016-mcp-connectors.md)). ([PR #29](https://github.com/PATELOM925/P-Dot/pull/29))
  - Problem: P-Dot cannot store a connection to Notion, Linear or Todoist.
  - Resolution: migration `20261031000000_mcp_connectors.sql` (expand) lets `connected_accounts` hold `notion`, `linear` and `todoist` rows with read scopes only, no address and a sealed bundle of at most 12,288 characters. An anonymous session cannot insert or update such a row. Google rows and the live Google code do not change. The SQL gate checks it in both grant modes.

- **Sign in with Google, and connect Google for Gmail and Calendar** ([decision 0014](docs/decisions/0014-google-sign-in-and-connection.md)). ([PR #25](https://github.com/PATELOM925/P-Dot/pull/25))
  - Problem: a user can sign in only with an email and a password, and P-Dot cannot read the user's Gmail or calendar or act there after approval.
  - Resolution:
    - "Continue with Google" on the login and signup pages uses Supabase OAuth with PKCE and a new `/auth/callback` route. Every failure gives one message, so it does not show whether an account exists.
    - Connect and Disconnect on the Connectors page run a separate Google consent. The state and the PKCE verifier are in a sealed httpOnly cookie bound to the user. The refresh token is stored sealed with AES-256-GCM under a Worker key, bound to the user and the provider. Disconnect revokes the token at Google, then deletes the row.
    - `src/server/google/` has the token store and the Gmail and Calendar calls with plain fetch, timeouts and zod, for the tool loop to use later. The Connectors page has a Google card with each state and a status message that takes focus.
    - `/api/health` reports `google`. The settings are optional; when they are missing, Google shows "Not set up".
    - Approved `send_email` and `create_calendar_event` proposals run through the decision route. The route checks Google access before the claim, calls Google once with the claim it won, and stores the outcome with `finish_external_action`. A timeout or a 5xx answers 202 and leaves the outcome unknown. `POST /api/actions/:id/recover` checks a calendar event again (Google refuses a second copy) or stores the user's answer. An email is never sent again by P-Dot. The approval card shows every field in full and has no Edit for these kinds. `tests/unit/external-actions-route.test.ts` has a write-fault sweep for both kinds, through the retry and the recovery calls, and the repository runs against the real functions in the gate.
    - Review round 1 ([review](https://github.com/PATELOM925/P-Dot/pull/25#issuecomment-6514025046)): an `executing` action shows its card and recovery controls on Today and in chat after a reload; the card re-renders when the lease ends; Disconnect deletes only the row it revoked, and keeps a connection that another tab made; the `To` header folds; anonymous demo sessions get 403 `account_required` for every Google action.
- **Replies use kept memories, the assistant can search the user's documents and propose tasks, reminders and memories, and new chats get titles from the model** ([decision 0013](docs/decisions/0013-ai-tools-and-scope.md)). ([PR #24](https://github.com/PATELOM925/P-Dot/pull/24))
  - Problem: replies did not use memories or documents, the context record always had empty memory and document lists, nothing created proposals for the approval cards, and titles came only from the first message.
  - Resolution:
    - The top 4 active memories of the mode go in the final user turn as an untrusted block. The embedding holds spend, and the first hold prices the block.
    - The own-data tools are offered only when the message asks to remind, schedule, add a task, remember or save, or asks about the user's notes, documents or memories (`detectToolIntent`, a pure rule check next to the router). Such a turn runs on the standard tier, and the line under the reply says that tools are on. A plain message stays on the fast tier with no tools.
    - `search_documents` and `search_memories` search the current mode, at most 5 matches, shown by title. Their IDs go in the context record.
    - `propose_task`, `propose_reminder` and `save_memory` write proposals or a proposed memory before `done`. A new message always gets its cards, also when two tabs send the same text at the same time. A "Retry reply" or a resend of a saved message writes nothing when its first attempt already wrote a proposal or a memory, also after the user edited, approved or rejected that card. If another user message in the conversation has the same stored time, the repeat writes, because the two turns cannot be told apart.
    - A short fast-tier call with a spend hold makes the title of a new conversation and sends a `title` event.
    - The chat route's write-fault sweep now also covers the proposal, memory and title writes: a resend or Retry reply does not repeat a proposal or a memory. A second sweep starts from a Retry reply of a saved message, so the reads that find earlier rows also fail in it.
    - After `done`, chat shows no error for a title event, Stop or a cut stream, and the composer is free at once. A late end of an earlier stream cannot free a newer send.
    - A generated title never overwrites a rename by the user: the rename applies only while the title is still the first one.
    - 10 hallucination guardrail tests on main's fakes, and updated screen copy.
    - One-time effect: a chat tab opened before this deploy skips the new `title` event, but its message box stays busy after the reply until the title step ends (at most 15 seconds). A reload fixes it.
- **Database step for Google sign-in and the Google connection** ([decision 0014](docs/decisions/0014-google-sign-in-and-connection.md)). ([PR #23](https://github.com/PATELOM925/P-Dot/pull/23))
  - Problem: P-Dot cannot store a connection to a user's Google account, and an action that acts outside P-Dot (send an email, create an event) has no safe path. The database cannot call Google, so the decision function cannot run such an action in its own transaction, and a retry must never send twice.
  - Resolution:
    - Migration `20261030000000_google_connection.sql` only adds or widens. It adds `connected_accounts` (one encrypted refresh token for each user, under RLS) with `save_connected_account`.
    - It adds the kinds `send_email` and `create_calendar_event` and the status `executing`. An approval claims the proposal; `finish_external_action` stores the outcome only for the current claim. `reclaim_external_action` and `settle_unknown_external_action` take a row out of `executing`; an email is never sent again by P-Dot.
    - The log triggers still write the action log, with IDs and no address, subject or body.
    - Review round 1 ([review](https://github.com/PATELOM925/P-Dot/pull/23#issuecomment-6513467206), [second review](https://github.com/PATELOM925/P-Dot/pull/23#issuecomment-6513510362)): an empty or NULL finish result is now `invalid`. A claim is bound to the connected Google account (`account_subject`, Google's `sub`) and its target, and recovery stops with `account_changed` when the account changed. An approval with no connected account answers `not_connected`. Only the request that wins a claim gets its ID, and `authenticated` cannot select it. The owner keeps "Confirm email" on and checks the accounts made while it was off before Google sign-in. `supabase/config.toml` gets a Google block for the local stack.
    - Review round 2 ([review](https://github.com/PATELOM925/P-Dot/pull/23#issuecomment-6513637406)): after a reclaim, a failure is never stored as a verified `failed`. The row keeps `executing` with `execution_outcome_unknown`, and only the user's answer settles it (`execution_attempt` counts the attempts). The account gate in `docs/DEPLOYMENT.md` is a checklist: delete or ban each account that is not proven safe; keep one only after the verified mailbox owner finished a password change and its sessions are revoked.
    - `tests/integration/sql-gate-google.test.ts` checks all of this in both grant modes, and main's repositories on the new schema. `docs/DEPLOYMENT.md` has the deploy steps and the owner's Google setup.

### Added
- **Model routing for Nemotron on Nebius Token Factory** ([PR #3](https://github.com/PATELOM925/P-Dot/pull/3), merged as `6e5224d`).
  - Problem: one fixed model for every message was either too slow and costly for small questions or too weak for hard ones. A provider error ended the reply with no second try.
  - Resolution: a rule-based router picks a tier with no extra model call:
    - fast: Lightning → Nano → Super
    - standard: Super → Lightning
    - heavy: Ultra → Super, with a thinking budget
    - vision: Super VL
  - Retries happen only before the first visible token. The router uses backoff with jitter, honors `Retry-After`, caps the attempts, sends one hedged request on the fast tier only, and skips a failing model for a short time with a circuit breaker.
  - Every attempt goes through the spend guard. Each reply shows which model answered and why.
  - Decision: [0007](docs/decisions/0007-model-routing.md).
- **Fixed during review (PR #3):**
  - A failed hedge reset the other model's retry wait, so it retried too early. Each model now keeps its own wait. ([round 1](https://github.com/PATELOM925/P-Dot/pull/3#issuecomment-6257890039))
  - Error events inside the stream were misread. "Model not found" stopped instead of moving to the next model, and "out of credit" was retried. Errors are now classified by status and label. ([round 1](https://github.com/PATELOM925/P-Dot/pull/3#issuecomment-6257890039))
  - Chat failed if the code went live before its migration. Reads and inserts now fall back to the old columns, for a missing route column only.
